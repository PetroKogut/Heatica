Drop Lähde outdoor spa pool is designed and made in Finland and guarantees a first-class bathing experience.

The name Lähde is Finnish and refers to a spring. The name was chosen to describe this pool package and to honour Finland’s clean nature and crystal-clear springs. Clean water and atmospheric bubbles well forth from the bottom of the pool just like in a spring. Finland’s largest spring is Sulaoja spring in Lapland’s Utsjoki. The Sami people have long considered spring Sulaoja as holy, and they believe that the spring has healing properties. We hope that Drop Lähde is a place where you can relax in the middle of your busy day.

The pool technology is assembled and water-tested at the factory.

The electrically-heated Drop pools are designed for year-round use and can be kept ready to use no matter the weather. Just open up the cover and enjoy!
