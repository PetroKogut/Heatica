Same amount of luxury in a smaller size. Drop Compact Series brings you the perfect combination of luxury and relaxation for smaller spaces.

 

With massage seats for 3, and 24 massage jets, Drop Vuolle Compact brings you the same experience as our flagship models, in a smaller package, but without compromising our signature design and combination of features and adaptability.

 

As with all Drop spas, Drop Vuolle Compact can also be embedded, fully or partially, to fit perfectly into your terrace or patio. Or you can install it on top of your terrace and cover the sides with beautiful paneling.
